Privacy Policy

Spending · Last updated July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Spending app ("the app") handles information. Spending is a personal budgeting tool operated by an individual, for personal use, to view their own bank-account spending.

Information the app accesses

With your explicit consent through Plaid, the app accesses your bank-account transaction data — transaction descriptions, amounts, dates, categories, and account/institution names. The app connects to your bank exclusively through Plaid Inc. Your banking login credentials are entered directly into Plaid and are never seen, handled, or stored by this app.

How the information is used

Your transaction data is used solely to display your own spending back to you — totals, category breakdowns, top merchants, and trends. It is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising or marketing.

How it is stored and secured

Data is stored in Convex, a SOC 2 Type II–compliant cloud database. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. The access tokens used to retrieve your data are stored server-side only and are never exposed to the browser or the app interface.

Data sharing

Your financial data is not shared with anyone except the service providers required to run the app: Plaid (to connect your bank) and Convex (to store your data). Your data is not sold.

Data retention and deletion

You can disconnect a bank at any time from within the app. Doing so removes that bank's connection and deletes its transactions from the app's database.

Plaid

This app uses Plaid to connect to your financial institution. Plaid's handling of your information is governed by Plaid's End User Privacy Policy, available at plaid.com/legal.
